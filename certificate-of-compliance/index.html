HomeCertificates of compliance

Certificates of compliance.

A purchaser's solicitor will require evidence that any extensions, conversions, or outbuildings were constructed in accordance with the relevant planning and building regulation requirements. A Certificate of Compliance provides this confirmation. We inspect the property, review it against the planning file, and issue an opinion that the solicitor can rely on.

01What it covers

Three questions a certificate answers.

Most sales need more than one. We tell you exactly which ones your property needs before we start.

  • 01.1

    Compliance with planning permission

    Does the building match the permission that was granted, and were its conditions met?

  • 01.2

    Exemption from planning

    We will clarify whether works carried out without planning permission comply with the planning exemption criteria.

  • 01.3

    Compliance with Building Regulations

    Does the work meet building regulation standards?

02How we do it

Inspected, checked, then signed.

  1. 01

    Send the details

    The address, any planning reference numbers and any drawings you have. If you have nothing, that's fine.

  2. 02

    We check the planning history

    We look up the property's planning file and read every permission and condition that applies.

  3. 03

    We inspect the property

    We measure the work on site and compare it against the approved drawings and the conditions.

  4. 04

    A signed opinion, or a clear list

    You get a signed opinion on compliance, ready for the contract pack.

If something doesn't comply, we will assess the situation and suggest the appropriate remedies.
