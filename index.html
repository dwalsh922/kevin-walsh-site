  • Attic conversionAn opinion on compliance with Building Regulations. If the roof shape changed, an opinion on planning status also.
  • Garden roomAn opinion on whether works are exempt development or in compliance with planning.
  • Front porchAn opinion on whether works are exempt development or in compliance with planning.
  • Garage conversionAn opinion on planning and building regulation status.
  • Internal alterationsUsually exempt from planning. However, if walls were removed or rooms rearranged, an opinion on compliance with Building Regulations, with regard to structure.
  • Changes to building elementsAn opinion on whether works are exempt development or in compliance with planning.
  • Changes to the site entranceAn opinion on whether works are exempt development or in compliance with planning. Note, widening an entrance onto a public road and dishing the kerb requires permission.
  • Front garden paved overAn opinion on whether works are exempt development or in compliance with planning.
  • Garden shedAn opinion on whether works are exempt development or in compliance with planning.
  • External canopies/awningsAn opinion on whether works are exempt development or in compliance with planning.

Four steps. No surprises.
